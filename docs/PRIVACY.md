# Privacy Policy — Car History

**Last updated:** 2026-08-09

**Operator:** Alexander Bykovskiy  
**Contact:** via [GitHub profile](https://github.com/alexander-bykovskiy) or [repository issues](https://github.com/alexander-bykovskiy/car-history/issues)  
**App:** Car History (`car_history`) — offline-first vehicle expense and maintenance tracker  
**Public copy of this policy:** https://github.com/alexander-bykovskiy/car-history/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md

## Summary

Car History stores your data **only on your device**. The app does **not** create accounts, does **not** sync to our servers, and does **not** include advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use the app, it may store locally (SQLite and app preferences):

- Vehicle details (brand, model/year, odometer, color, optional photo)
- Fuelings, maintenance records, parts, catalogs (fuel types, stations, services, etc.)
- Reminders and display preferences (units, currency, tips)
- Backup files only if **you** export them

## Photo library access

If you add a car photo, the app may ask for photo library access to let you choose an image. The image is stored locally in the app database. We do not upload it.

## Backups

Export and import are initiated only by you. An exported backup file may contain the same local data listed above (including photos encoded in the file). We do not receive that file unless you choose to share it with someone.

## Data we do not collect

We do not operate a backend for this app and do not collect:

- Account credentials
- Location tracking for advertising
- Analytics or crash-reporting identifiers from third-party SDKs (none are bundled for that purpose)

## How to delete data

- Delete individual cars or records inside the app
- Clear or remove exported backup files you created
- Uninstall the app to remove its local database and preferences on that device

## Children

Car History is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date and this file in the public repository will reflect the current version.

## Contact

For privacy questions about local data in Car History, contact the operator through the GitHub links above.
